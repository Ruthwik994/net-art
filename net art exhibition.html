Human distortion

The Collection Human distortion displays three poignant Net. Art videos that play on the distortion of the human psyche. Artwork 1, The Collapse of PAL, by Rosa Menken doesn’t follow a human in part of its narration, in fact, it follows an Algorithm and has themes of Angels. However, it follows human fears such as finality and its brutality, this further exemplified with presumed humanoid face of the algorithm, which is now distorted. Artwork 2, flo\/\/, 2011 by Małgosia Woźnica showcases the distortion of human figures, including distorted and warped pop-culture references and human art, almost invoking the short life of human efforts through the quick successions of different, warped, images. The final artwork, all Right, by Gabriel Lester seems to warp the viewers emotion rather than its subjects. Creating tension within the viewers mind through his use of lighting, imagery, and architecture.

The Collapse of PAL, 2011, by Rosa Menken. A Net. Art piece depicted in video, showcasing the termination of PAL through the concepts of The Angels’ history.

flo\/\/, 2011 by Małgosia Woźnica shows vibrant video distortions of human bodies, faces, or caricatures.

All Right, 2006, by Gabriel Lester tries to evoke “the suspension of disbelief on the part of the viewer”, [Ref.] warping the human experience of processing what you see.
